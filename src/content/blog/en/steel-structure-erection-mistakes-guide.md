The most common steel structure erection mistakes are anchor bolt misalignment, wrong lifting sequence, missing temporary bracing, undertightened bolts and mismarked members. Each fix depends on the site conditions and the drawings — not fixed global values. ZhongSai has built steel structures for 20 years and focused on exports for 15, providing installation technical guidance with the package.

This guide lists the mistakes, the consequences and the prevention.

![Steel structure erection site with crane and workers (illustrative concept image)](/images/blog/steel-structure-erection-mistakes-guide/cover.jpg)

## Part 1: The five mistakes that cause most site problems

| Mistake | Consequence | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor bolt misalignment | Columns do not fit, re-drilling on site | Check the bolt template against the drawings before pouring |
| Wrong lifting sequence | Members cannot close, rework | Follow the erection sequence in the drawings |
| Missing temporary bracing | Frame unstable during erection, collapse risk | Install temporary bracing before releasing the crane |
| Undertightened bolts | Joints slip under load | Torque high-strength bolts to the specified value |
| Mismarked members | Wrong member lifted, delays | Check the erection marks against the layout |

## Part 2: Check before erection starts

- Foundation position, level and anchor bolts checked against the drawings
- Member list and erection marks confirmed
- Lifting plan and crane capacity reviewed
- Temporary bracing and safety equipment ready
- Weather and site access confirmed

![Worker checking anchor bolts on the foundation (illustrative concept image)](/images/blog/steel-structure-erection-mistakes-guide/foundation.jpg)

## Part 3: Temporary bracing — why it matters

Until the frame is closed and permanently braced, the structure is not stable. Temporary bracing, guys and props keep the first bay plumb while the next members go up. The arrangement depends on the frame layout and the erection sequence — confirm with the engineer.

![Temporary bracing holding a steel frame bay during erection (illustrative concept image)](/images/blog/steel-structure-erection-mistakes-guide/bracing.jpg)

## Part 4: Bolt tightening on site

High-strength bolts are tightened in the specified sequence and to the specified torque. Undertightening is a common defect; so is tightening in the wrong order, which leaves some bolts loose. Use the calibrated tool and follow the drawing.

![Worker tightening high-strength bolts with a torque wrench (illustrative concept image)](/images/blog/steel-structure-erection-mistakes-guide/bolt.jpg)

## Part 5: Erection sequence and member storage

The erection sequence in the drawings decides which bay closes first. Members should be stored and stacked by bay, so the right member is lifted at the right time without re-handling.

![Steel members stacked by bay on the erection site (illustrative concept image)](/images/blog/steel-structure-erection-mistakes-guide/sequence.jpg)

## FAQ

### What is the most common mistake in steel structure erection?

Anchor bolt misalignment and wrong lifting sequence cause most site rework. Both are preventable by checking the drawings before work starts.

### Who is responsible for the erection?

The local contractor carries out the physical erection. ZhongSai provides the erection sequence and installation technical guidance for the steel package.

### Do I need temporary bracing?

Yes, until the frame is closed and permanently braced. The arrangement depends on the frame layout and sequence.

### How are anchor bolts checked?

Against the drawing before pouring: position, level, projection and template. Column bases are then set to match.

### Who tightens the high-strength bolts?

The local erection crew, following the specified sequence and torque in the drawings.

### What if a member does not fit on site?

Report the measurement to the supplier; check the member mark and the drawing first — most issues are solved by checking, not cutting.
