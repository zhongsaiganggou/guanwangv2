Importing steel structures — can you actually read the mill test certificate? The grade, heat number, chemistry and yield/tensile values are all on it, and acceptance means tracing the heat number to the physical steel and matching the grade to the drawings. EN 10204 3.1 vs 3.2 must be settled before ordering. ZhongSai coordinates MTC documentation for the structures it fabricates.
