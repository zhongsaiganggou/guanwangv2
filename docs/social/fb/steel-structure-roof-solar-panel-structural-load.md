Can a steel structure roof carry solar panels? Roof area alone is not enough — panels add self-weight, wind uplift and maintenance loads, and snow combines with them on cold sites. The load must travel through purlins, frames, columns and foundations. Including solar loads in a new building avoids later strengthening; retrofitting an existing roof needs an engineer to check the load path.
