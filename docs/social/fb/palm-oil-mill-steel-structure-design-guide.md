## 英文版（备用，可选发）

The expensive mistake in a palm oil mill is treating it as a big warehouse with machines inside: sterilizer cages, the threshing drum, the boiler house and kernel silos bring vibration, thermal movement and steam condensate that shake a copied warehouse frame apart joint by joint. Detailing before the process data arrives means redesigning on site — put the equipment list, dynamic loads and local design basis on the table first, then talk column spacing.
