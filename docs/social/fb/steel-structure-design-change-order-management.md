钢结构下单后还能改设计吗？可以，但要走书面变更单——变更越早影响越小，一旦材料已采购、构件已加工，就可能产生返工和费用、推迟工期。变更单要写清改什么、费用、工期变化，双方确认后再执行；最省麻烦的做法是加工前把图纸冻结、把门窗开洞和设备一次确认好。改图怎么算、怎么避免后期变更，这篇讲清楚：https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/zh/blog/steel-structure-design-change-order-management/ #钢结构 #钢结构厂房 #装配式建筑

===== FACEBOOK 帖子・ENGLISH (optional) =====

Can you change a steel building design after ordering? Yes, but it should go through a written change order — the earlier the change, the smaller the impact, and once material is ordered or members are fabricated, rework, cost and delays can follow. A change order should state what changes, the cost and schedule effect, and be confirmed before work starts; freezing the drawings and confirming doors, openings and equipment before fabrication avoids most changes. How changes are priced and how to avoid them: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/steel-structure-design-change-order-management/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
