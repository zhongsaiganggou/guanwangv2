Fire protection coating on steel is not "just painted on" — thickness and application conditions decide whether the fire rating holds. Intumescent is thin and paintable; cementitious is thick and rough. The core of acceptance is dry-film thickness, and edges and welds fail first. ZhongSai can provide technical guidance for the buildings we fabricate.
