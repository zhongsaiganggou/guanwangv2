## 英文版（备用、可选发）— 整段复制

===== FACEBOOK 帖子・ENGLISH (optional) =====

A door opening in a steel building is not a hole cut in the wall — it breaks the wall panel, so side columns and a lintel carry the load instead. Size, position and reinforcement must be fixed before fabrication, because moving a door after cutting means re-making the frame. Set the opening schedule by what passes through: forklift, truck, conveyor or people. Full guide: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/steel-structure-doors-openings-guide/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
