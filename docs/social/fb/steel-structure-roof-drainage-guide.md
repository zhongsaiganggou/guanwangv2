A leaking steel building is usually a drainage problem, not a panel problem. Gutter size depends on the roof catchment area and local rainfall, and downpipe spacing, fall and overflow are set by the design — guess it and the eaves overflow in a heavy storm. Get the drainage detail right and protect the structure for years.
