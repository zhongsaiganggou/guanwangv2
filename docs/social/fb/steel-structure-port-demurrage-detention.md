What happens if imported steel is not cleared or collected after the vessel arrives? Demurrage, detention and port storage charges accrue per day and per container, and they usually fall on the buyer under common import terms, unless the supplier's documents caused the delay. Free days and daily rates vary by port and carrier, so the reliable approach is complete documents, pre-arrival clearance, prompt duty payment and fast container return.
