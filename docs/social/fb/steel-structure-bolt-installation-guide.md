Steel bolts are not just "tightened" — high-strength bolts must reach a defined preload through the specified procedure: snug-tight first, then final tightening by torque, turn-of-nut or tension-control method. Under-tightened bolts slip and loosen; over-tightening damages the connection. Here's what buyers and site teams should check.
