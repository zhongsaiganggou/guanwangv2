## 英文版（备用、可选发）— 整段复制

===== FACEBOOK 帖子・ENGLISH (optional) =====

Can a specified steel grade or section be replaced if it is not available? Substitution is possible, but it is never decided on the shop floor: the substitute must meet or exceed the original design in strength, toughness, weldability and section properties, be approved in writing by the design engineer or buyer, and be recorded with an updated MTC so the material stays traceable. Matching tensile strength alone is not enough, sub-grade and thickness effects are also checked. How the request, approval and records work: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/steel-material-substitution-equivalent-approval/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
