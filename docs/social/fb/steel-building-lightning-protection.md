Is a steel building safe in a lightning storm? The steel itself can form part of the protection: a qualifying metal roof can act as an air terminal, steel columns as down conductors, and foundations or electrodes as the earth, so a continuous, grounded frame carries lightning current safely to earth. But the protection level, grounding resistance and surge protective devices have no universal number, they are set by a local electrical engineer to the local code.
