The longer a steel building is, the more one detail matters: expansion joints. Steel expands and contracts with temperature, and a long building without joints or temperature zones can build up stress in columns, connections, sheets and flashings, leading to buckled panels or leaks. There is no universal spacing, it depends on the structure, local temperature range and code, and common details use double columns, sliding connections, slotted purlins and flexible flashing.
