Misreading a steel drawing is expensive: wrong dimensions, wrong holes, wrong welds, rework. GA drawings show the layout, shop drawings show every member, erection drawings show the sequence, and the material schedule ties it together. Fabrication and erection drawings ship with the steel scope.
