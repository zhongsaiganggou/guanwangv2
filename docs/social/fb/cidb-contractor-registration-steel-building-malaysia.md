## 英文版（备用，可选发）

In Malaysia, a supplier's "CIDB certificate" and the erecting contractor's registration are two different documents held by two different parties — registration sets who may build, in which category, and up to what contract value, while product compliance is a separate check on the steel itself. This guide separates the three checks and adds a ten-point pre-award list: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/cidb-contractor-registration-steel-building-malaysia/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
