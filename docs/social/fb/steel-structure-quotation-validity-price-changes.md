Why does a steel structure quotation expire? It is not a permanent price — it is based on the steel price, exchange rate, freight and capacity at the time of the offer, and all of these move, so every quote has a validity period. Ordering within the window, arranging the agreed deposit to reserve material, and stating whether the price is fixed or adjustable in the contract reduces price surprises. What justifies a re-quote and how to lock a price: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/steel-structure-quotation-validity-price-changes/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
