Container or breakbulk for your steel structure? Both work — dismantled columns, beams and purlins usually fit 40HQ boxes, while long-span beams and assembled trusses often ship as breakbulk. The choice depends on the drawings, the destination port's crane capacity and the schedule. ZhongSai coordinates packing, container loading and export.
