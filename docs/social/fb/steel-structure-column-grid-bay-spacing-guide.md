How should a steel building column grid be set? More columns do not mean stronger, and wider bays are not always cheaper — wider bays mean fewer columns and foundations but larger crane beams, purlins and cladding rails. The right grid follows your production flow, equipment, crane capacity and future expansion, then a registered engineer confirms it against drawings. Get it wrong and removing columns, adding a crane or fitting solar later becomes costly.
