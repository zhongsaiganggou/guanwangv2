===== FACEBOOK 帖子・ENGLISH (optional) =====

How should an imported steel structure be paid? Each release — deposit, production, pre-shipment, arrival — should be tied to a verifiable milestone and document: drawing approval, mill certificates, inspection report, bill of lading. T/T and L/C suit different situations, and FOB/CIF define where risk transfers, not how you pay. Put the milestones and documents in the contract and both sides are protected.
