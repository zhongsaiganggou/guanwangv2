## 英文版（备用，可选发）

A conveyor gallery is not a box on legs — it is designed backwards from the belt: width, speed, starting tension, drive location and walkway access fix the section and the trestle spacing before anything else. Two things get missed: trestles must resist the belt's longitudinal pull, and an empty gallery is often governed by wind, not weight.
