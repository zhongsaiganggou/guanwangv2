## 英文版（备用，可选发）

A fabricated steel building entering Malaysia is a controlled construction product, not general cargo. The importer needs a Product Certification or a Full Type Test Report in place before the vessel sails, then a CIDB Certificate of Approval per consignment — documents cannot be recreated at the port. This guide sets out both routes, how mill certificates are accepted, port verification and the three-day approval window: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/cidb-coa-imported-steel-structures/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
