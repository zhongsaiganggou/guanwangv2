One member in the wrong bay costs an extra lift. Steel assembly marking links every member to its grid, level and type on the drawing, while match marks bind the two members that must be erected together — orientation, side and pairing written at the joint. This guide covers numbering logic, when shop trial assembly is worth the cost, how marks survive coating and sea transport, and what to check before shipment.
