===== FACEBOOK 帖子・ENGLISH (optional) =====

Do you need insurance when a container of steel crosses the ocean? Yes — Incoterms transfer risk, but they do not mean anyone actually bought cover. Under FOB or FCA the buyer usually arranges insurance, while under CIF or CIP the seller generally arranges only minimum cover and wider protection needs to be added separately. What is covered, what is excluded, how the insured value is set, and how to claim if cargo arrives damaged should all be clear before shipment, not after an incident: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/steel-structure-cargo-insurance-import-guide/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
