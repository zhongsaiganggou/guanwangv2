Adding a second floor changes the structural system, not just the height: lateral load travels through floor diaphragms into a designed lateral system, column bases need real fixity, and vibration, fire compartmentation and egress start to drive member sizes. Until the local design inputs — grid, code, approving engineer, fire strategy — are fixed, any multi-storey quotation is priced on assumptions.
