## 英文版（备用、可选发）— 整段复制

===== FACEBOOK 帖子・ENGLISH (optional) =====

Does a steel floor that feels like it moves when you walk mean it is unsafe? Perceptible movement and strength are separate things: long-span, lightweight composite floors can be noticed under walking while still meeting the code, and comfort is checked separately. It mainly comes down to stiffness, natural frequency and acceleration, and can be controlled through member size, floor mass, layout, and where needed damping or equipment isolation, with no universal limit, a structural engineer judges it to the use and local code. Why it happens and how it is controlled: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/steel-floor-vibration-human-comfort/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
