## 英文版（备用、可选发）— 整段复制

===== FACEBOOK 帖子・ENGLISH (optional) =====

Importing steel by tonnage — why don't theoretical weight and weighbridge weight match? The gap comes from mill tolerance, negative tolerance, welds, stiffeners, coating and surface condition; small per metre, but it adds up across a truck. The contract must state which weight basis is used, whether re-weighing is allowed and how differences are handled. ZhongSai states the weight basis on quotation and packing list.
