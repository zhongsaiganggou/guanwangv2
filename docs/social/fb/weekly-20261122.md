This week we took the "process-plant steel structure" question apart piece by piece: how a mezzanine ties into an existing frame, where the scope ends on water treatment tank covers and small equipment rooms, who owns the boiler supports and waste bunker on biomass and waste-to-energy plants, and the parameter list buyers in Malaysia and Indonesia should align before ordering. Three takeaways worth keeping: for mezzanines and bunkers, settle the load path and who checks the structure before discussing tonnage; on plant projects, write the interface between steel supply and boiler, flue gas and crane equipment into a table instead of leaving it vague in the quotation; and for export procurement, lock the standard, grade, certificate type and unloading conditions early, because the later the parameters are aligned, the more drawings get revised.
