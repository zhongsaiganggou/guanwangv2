What does a steel building warranty actually cover? Usually only the agreed manufacturing and material defects, such as welding, fabrication or non-conforming members; shipping damage, improper erection, rust from outdoor storage, later design changes, overload or lack of maintenance are generally outside it. There is no universal warranty period, so the scope and period must be written into the contract, and inspecting on arrival, taking dated photos and checking against the packing list makes the cause clear.
