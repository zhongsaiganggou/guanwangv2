What are the multi-level steel frames carrying pipes in a petrochemical plant? They are steel pipe racks, the main route for process pipes and cable trays, formed by transverse bents, longitudinal bracing, multi-level beams, walkways and guardrails. They differ from buildings: usually open, multi-level, carrying pipe and equipment loads, and the pipes expand with temperature, so supports and guides are coordinated early. How a rack is structured, what it carries and what to coordinate: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/steel-pipe-rack-structure/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
