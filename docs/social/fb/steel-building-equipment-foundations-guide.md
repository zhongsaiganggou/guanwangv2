Heavy machines should not just sit on a concrete slab: a registered local engineer must confirm the slab and ground can carry the load. Equipment foundations are usually designed locally, but machine loads, anchor positions and pits must be coordinated with the building frame before fabrication — send the equipment layout with your drawings.
