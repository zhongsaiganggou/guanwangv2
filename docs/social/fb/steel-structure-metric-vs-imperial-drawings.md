An inch-pound drawing sent to a metric shop is not a translation job. 1/4 in plate becoming 6 mm, A325 becoming 10.9, a 1/16 in tolerance read as 1.5 mm — each of those is a design change, usually made without a signature. This guide separates what is arithmetic, what needs approval, and what belongs in a conversion register before the first plate is cut.
