屋面檩条选 C 型还是 Z 型？看跨度、坡度和荷载：Z 型搭接连续、长跨效率高，门式刚架屋面常用；C 型简单、短跨和墙梁更顺手。檩条规格和搭接长度要按实际图纸荷载算，没有一刀切的标准。中赛钢构做钢结构20年、专注出口15年，檩条跟框架、围护一起配套供货，按你的图纸协调规格。查看完整对比：https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/zh/blog/purlin-selection-c-vs-z/ #钢结构 #钢结构厂房 #装配式建筑

C purlin or Z purlin for your roof? Z sections lap for continuous multi-span roofs and suit longer spans; C sections are simpler for short spans and wall girts. Sizes and laps are calculated from the actual drawings and loads, not a one-size rule. ZhongSai supplies purlins with the frame and cladding package.
