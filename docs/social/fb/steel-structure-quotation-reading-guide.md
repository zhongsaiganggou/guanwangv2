## 中文版（主用，整段复制）

拿到钢结构报价，先别急着比总价。同一个厂房报出两个相差很大的数字，很多时候不是谁在乱报，而是一家按出厂价、理论重量、只做底漆，另一家按 FOB、含包装配件和完整涂装体系。先看供什么、再看怎么计量、最后看排除了什么——基础、安装吊车、清关税费、现场补漆这些，通常都不在钢材价里。我们把这十个报价行和九项常见漏项拆开写了，还附了一张两家同口径对比的表。看全文：https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/zh/blog/steel-structure-quotation-reading-guide/ #钢结构 #钢结构厂房 #装配式建筑

## English (optional)

Two quotations for the same steel building can differ widely without either supplier being dishonest: one is supply-only at theoretical weight with a primer, the other is FOB with packing, accessories and a full coating system. Read the document in order — scope, weight basis, exclusions — before comparing totals. We broke down the ten lines every quotation should contain, the nine items most often excluded, and a bid-leveling method for comparing suppliers fairly: https://zhongsai-steelstructure.com/en/blog/steel-structure-quotation-reading-guide/ #SteelStructure #SteelBuilding #PrefabBuilding
